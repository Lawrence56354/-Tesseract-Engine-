4D ENGINE CONTROLS:
• 3D Movement: W/S (Up/Down), A/D (Left/Right), Q/E (Forward/Backward)
• 4D W-Axis Shift: R (Move Positive W), F (Move Negative W)
• 4D Rotations: 1/2 (XW rotation), 3/4 (YW rotation), 5/6 (ZW rotation)
Notice how shifting on the W-axis makes parts of the cube shrink or expand toward the center point!
